Snorkeling in Hikkaduwa Coral Reef

Discover vibrant living coral gardens and schools of tropical fish inside the sheltered marine sanctuary.

The Hikkaduwa Coral Sanctuary Experience

The Hikkaduwa Coral Reef is Sri Lanka's premier marine sanctuary, famous for its calm shallow lagoon shelter, rich biodiversity, and tropical waters. We provide sanitized masks, dry snorkels, fins, life jackets, and a local guide who knows where the clearest visibility and marine life gather.

Non-swimmers and kids can comfortably participate using our buoyant flotation vests while guides guide them safely around the reef.
