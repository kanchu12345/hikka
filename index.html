Discover All Ocean Adventures & Marine Wildlife

From the protected shallows of the National Coral Reef Sanctuary to the open blue depths of the Indian Ocean, Hikkaduwa is a world-class marine haven. Snorkel among kaleidoscopic coral gardens, swim with resident wild green sea turtles at Turtle Beach, hunt game fish offshore, or cruise the mangrove channels of Madu River — all hosted by certified native skippers.
