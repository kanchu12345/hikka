Sri Lanka’s Top-Rated Surf School

Hikka Surf School is rated 5.0 out of 5 from verified Google reviews on Narigama Beach, Hikkaduwa. We run daily beginner, private, and progression surf lessons led by passionate, certified local instructors. With maximum four students per coach or private 1-on-1 training, you get immediate feedback on every wave. The south coast surf season runs all year with warm 28 °C tropical water so no wetsuit is needed, and lessons start at just $25.
