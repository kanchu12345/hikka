Mirissa Whale Watching

Encounter giant Blue Whales, Sperm Whales, and acrobatic Spinner Dolphins in the deep Indian Ocean.

Available on request (Nov - April season) — arranged with certified coast-guard registered Mirissa boat partners.

Mirissa Whale Watching Package ($65 USD)

Sri Lanka's southern coast is one of the world's premier locations for spotting Blue Whales. We arrange early morning hotel pickup from Hikkaduwa, comfortable AC drive to Mirissa Harbour, and return transport after the boat cruise.

  • ✓ Hotel pickup & drop-off in Hikkaduwa (approx 5:30 AM)
  • ✓ Coast Guard approved passenger vessel ticket
  • ✓ Light breakfast snack & bottled water onboard
  • ✓ High sighting probability during Nov - April peak season
