Must-Do Wildlife Experience

Turtle Experiences in Hikkaduwa

Swim alongside gentle giant green turtles in their natural ocean environment.

Ethical Wild Turtle Encounters

Hikkaduwa is famous worldwide for the wild giant sea turtles that visit the shallow bay right in front of the beach every day. Observe these majestic creatures grazing on seaweed in crystal-clear water. We emphasize respectful, ethical wildlife interactions with zero feeding or disturbing.
