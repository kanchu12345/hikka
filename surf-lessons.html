About Our Surf Coaching

Hikkaduwa offers some of Sri Lanka's finest wave setups, from gentle sandy beach breaks ideal for first-timers to peeling reef breaks for improving surfers. Our certified local surf coaches provide in-depth ocean safety, paddling technique, wave reading, and pop-up coaching in the water right beside you.

Every session starts on the beach with pop-up technique practice and wave dynamics before paddling out together into waist-deep water. We stay by your side on every single wave to push you into the right spot and guide your stance.
