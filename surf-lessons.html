Essential Knowledge

Basic Surfing Guidelines & Safety

Everything first-timers and intermediate surfers need to know before stepping into the ocean in Hikkaduwa.

🛡️

1. Safety & Wipeout Rules

  • ✓
    Always Cover Your Head: When wiping out, protect your head with both arms before surfacing to avoid hitting your board or fins.
  • ✓
    Fall Flat (Starfish Fall): Always fall flat on the surface like a starfish. Never dive headfirst or jump straight down.
  • ✓
    Never Ditch Your Board: Hold firmly onto your board or use the leash. Never let go if other swimmers or surfers are behind you.
  • ✓
    Riptide Awareness: If you feel a seaward current, stay calm on your board and paddle parallel to the beach, not straight against it.
💬¡ Coach Tip: Relax and stay calm during wipeouts"”it's part of the fun!
🌊Š

2. Surfing Etiquette in the Water

  • ✓
    Right of Way: The surfer closest to the breaking peak / curl of the wave has priority. Give them full room to ride.
  • ✓
    Do Not "Drop In": Never take off on a wave if another surfer is already riding it toward you.
  • ✓
    Do Not "Snake": Don't paddle around someone waiting patiently just to steal inside priority.
  • ✓
    Paddle Wide: When paddling back out, paddle in the channel or wide around the breaking zone to avoid colliding with riding surfers.
🏄™ Golden Rule: Respect the ocean, local surfers, and fellow learners.
â˜€ï¸

3. Preparation & Sun Care

  • ✓
    UV 50+ Rash Guard: We provide UV protective rash vests to protect your skin from sun and board friction.
  • ✓
    Reef-Safe Zinc Sunblock: Sri Lanka's tropical sun is strong. Apply thick zinc on cheeks, nose, and lips before entering.
  • ✓
    Leash Attached to Back Ankle: Secure your safety leash snugly around the ankle of your back (dominant) foot.
  • ✓
    Warm Up & Hydration: Complete a 3-minute beach stretch and drink water or king coconut before and after your session.
🥥 Recovery: Free fresh water showers & cold coconut available after lessons.

✅ DO (Best Practices)

Listen closely to your coach, look up at the beach while popping up (not down at your feet), bend your knees (not your waist), and smile on every single wave!

âŒ DON'T (Things to Avoid)

Don't hold your board sideways in front of you against the waves, don't grab the rails with flat palms, and don't panic if you fall – the soft sand is just beneath you.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I learn to surf if I cannot swim well?

Yes! Beginner lessons take place in waist-deep water on a soft sandy beach. Your instructor stays right next to you the entire time holding your board. We also have safety life vests available.

What should I bring for my surf lesson?

Just bring your swimwear and a towel! We provide the surfboard, UV rash guard, reef-safe zinc sunscreen, and a freshwater shower after your session.

What time of day is best for surf lessons?

Early mornings (06:30 AM to 09:30 AM) and late afternoons (04:00 PM to 06:00 PM) are best because winds are glassy and the ocean is calmest.

How do I book? Do I need to pay in advance?

You can book instantly on WhatsApp! No advance payment required for single lessons"”you can pay cash (USD, EUR, GBP, or LKR) or card when you arrive at the beach.
