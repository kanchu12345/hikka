🌴 SRI LANKA • SOUTHERN PROVINCE

Top Tourist Destinations & Attractions

From the golden surf sandbanks of Hikkaduwa and UNESCO colonial ramparts of Galle Fort to wild leopards in Yala and whale watching in Mirissa — explore the best of Southern Sri Lanka with our trusted local drivers and guides.

EXPLORE THE SOUTH COAST

Southern Province Highlights

Home Base

🌊 Hikkaduwa & Turtle Beach

Sri Lanka's iconic surf hub with beginner-friendly sandbank waves, shallow reef giant sea turtle encounters, vibrant beachside dining, and coral marine sanctuary.

  • ✓ Narigama Beach Surf Break
  • ✓ Wild Sea Turtle Reef (Turtle Beach)
  • ✓ National Coral Reef Marine Sanctuary
  • ✓ Seenigama Island Temple
UNESCO World Heritage

🏰 Galle Dutch Fort

Just 25 mins from Hikkaduwa. 17th-century fortified colonial fortress featuring cobblestone lanes, boutique cafes, the historic white lighthouse, and breathtaking rampart sunsets.

  • ✓ Galle Lighthouse & Flag Rock
  • ✓ Dutch Colonial Architecture & Museum
  • ✓ Rampart Sunset Walk & Ocean Views
  • ✓ Boutique Shopping & Art Galleries
Whale Watching Hub

🐋 Mirissa Beach

World-famous destination for Blue Whale and dolphin watching expeditions, the iconic Coconut Tree Hill viewpoint, and picturesque Secret Beach.

  • ✓ Blue Whale & Dolphin Safaris
  • ✓ Iconic Coconut Tree Hill
  • ✓ Parrot Rock & Secret Beach
  • ✓ Fresh Seafood Beachside Dining
Calm Bay & Snorkel

🏖️ Unawatuna & Jungle Beach

A crescent-shaped golden bay with calm swimming waters, hidden Jungle Beach, and the hilltop Japanese Peace Pagoda overlooking Galle harbour.

  • ✓ Japanese Peace Pagoda Hilltop
  • ✓ Hidden Jungle Beach Snorkeling
  • ✓ Safe Year-Round Calm Swimming
  • ✓ Beach Clubs & Sunset Bars
Mangrove Wetland

🚤 Madu River & Bentota

A biodiverse wetland sanctuary with 64 islands. Cruise through dense mangrove tunnels, visit Cinnamon Island, and experience natural river fish therapy.

  • ✓ Mangrove Cave Boat Safari
  • ✓ Cinnamon Peeling Demonstration
  • ✓ Ancient Island Monastery
  • ✓ Bentota Water Sports (Jet Ski / Banana)
Surf & Culture

🏄 Weligama & Stilt Fishermen

Known for sandy wave breaks, famous traditional stilt fishermen along the coastal reefs of Ahangama & Koggala, and serene lake temples.

  • ✓ Traditional Stilt Fishermen Photography
  • ✓ Weligama Sandy Surf Beach
  • ✓ Midigama Reef Breaks (Lazy Left / Rams)
  • ✓ Koggala Lake & Martin Wickramasinghe Museum
Wildlife Safari

🐆 Yala National Park

The premier wildlife park in Asia with the highest leopard density in the world. Full day 4x4 open-top jeep safari with private driver from Hikkaduwa.

  • ✓ Sri Lankan Leopard Tracking
  • ✓ Wild Asian Elephant Herds
  • ✓ Sloth Bears, Crocodiles & Birds
  • ✓ Private A/C Transport Included
UNESCO Rainforest

🌿 Sinharaja Rainforest

The last primary tropical rainforest in Sri Lanka. Trek with certified naturalist trackers through giant tree canopies, hidden waterfalls, and exotic endemic birds.

  • ✓ UNESCO World Biosphere Reserve
  • ✓ Jungle Trekking & Natural Waterfalls
  • ✓ Endemic Bird & Butterfly Watching
  • ✓ Professional Forest Tracker Guide
