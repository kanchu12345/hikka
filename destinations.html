📍 Galle District 0 Mins (Our Base)

🌊 Hikkaduwa & Turtle Beach

Sri Lanka’s legendary surf and marine hub. Renowned for consistent sandbank wave breaks at Narigama, shallow reef encounters with wild giant Green Sea Turtles, vibrant coral sanctuary snorkeling, and colorful beachfront dining.

✓ Narigama Beach Beginner & Pro Surf Breaks
✓ Wild Sea Turtle Reef (Turtle Beach)
✓ National Coral Reef Marine Sanctuary
✓ Seenigama Island Temple & Tsunami Memorial
Surf Lessons: $25 | Snorkeling: $20
UNESCO World Heritage
📍 Galle District ⏱️ 25 Mins Drive

🏰 Galle Dutch Fort & Lighthouse

A living 17th-century fortified colonial citadel founded by the Portuguese and expanded by the Dutch. Stroll atop historic ocean ramparts, visit the iconic white lighthouse, browse artisan jewelry shops, and enjoy stylish colonial cafes.

✓ Iconic Galle Lighthouse & Flag Rock Sunset
✓ Dutch Reformed Church & Maritime Museum
✓ Pedlar Street Boutiques, Gelato & Gem Stores
✓ Cobblestone Colonial Streets & Clock Tower
Private Car & Driver: From $35
Calm Golden Bay
📍 Galle District ⏱️ 35 Mins Drive

🏖️ Unawatuna & Jungle Beach

A world-famous crescent-shaped golden sand bay with calm, year-round swimming waters. Features the secluded Jungle Beach snorkeling cove and the hilltop Japanese Peace Pagoda on Rumassala Cliff.

✓ Japanese Peace Pagoda Hilltop Ocean View
✓ Hidden Jungle Beach Snorkeling Cove
✓ Rumassala Nature Reserve & Coral Reefs
✓ Lively Beachfront Dining & Sunset Lounges
Private Car & Driver: From $40
Iconic Heritage & Surf
📍 Galle / Matara Coast ⏱️ 45 Mins Drive

🎣 Koggala Stilt Fishermen & Lake

Photograph Sri Lanka's world-famous traditional stilt fishermen perched over breaking coastal coral reefs. Take a tranquil catamaran boat ride across sprawling Koggala Lake and visit the Martin Wickramasinghe Folk Museum.

✓ Traditional Stilt Fishermen Photography
✓ Koggala Lake Boat Safari & Spice Island
✓ Martin Wickramasinghe Cultural Folk Museum
✓ Ahangama & Midigama World-Class Surf Breaks
Private Car & Driver: From $45
Gentle Sandy Bay
📍 Matara District ⏱️ 50 Mins Drive

🏄 Weligama Bay & Taprobane

A massive sheltered crescent bay with soft sandy seafloor, making it one of the most sheltered and beginner-friendly sandy surf bays in Sri Lanka. View the private Taprobane Island villa and taste fresh catches at the bustling beach fish market.

✓ Gentle Sandbank Surf Lessons for Beginners
✓ Scenic View of Taprobane Private Island
✓ Fresh Seafood Beach Stalls & Cozy Surf Cafes
✓ Kushtarajagala Ancient Rock Carving
Private Car & Driver: From $45
Whale Watching & Viewpoint
📍 Matara District ⏱️ 55 Mins Drive

🌴 Mirissa & Coconut Tree Hill

The Southern Coast’s premier whale-watching haven for Blue Whales, Sperm Whales, and pods of spinner dolphins. Walk to the Instagram-famous Coconut Tree Hill palm headland, explore Secret Beach, and climb Parrot Rock.

✓ Blue Whale & Dolphin Safari Ocean Boat Tour
✓ Iconic Coconut Tree Hill Panoramic Palm View
✓ Parrot Rock Lookout & Hidden Secret Beach
✓ Beachfront Candlelight Seafood Barbeques
Whale Watching: $55 | Day Trip: $50
Barrier Reef Lagoon
📍 Matara District ⏱️ 1 Hr 10 Mins

🐢 Polhena Beach & Madiha

Protected by an offshore coral barrier reef, Polhena forms a tranquil natural saltwater swimming pool where wild green sea turtles graze close to shore. Neighboring Madiha is a stylish hipster hub for reef surf and cocktails.

✓ Safe Calm Swimming Inside Protected Reef
✓ Wild Sea Turtle Snorkeling in Shallow Waters
✓ Madiha Point Break Surfing & Sunset Cafes
✓ Matara Star Fort & Nilwala River Bridge
Private Car & Driver: From $55
Southernmost Point of Sri Lanka
📍 Southernmost Tip ⏱️ 1 Hr 20 Mins

🗼 Dondra Head Lighthouse & Matara

Stand at the southernmost geographic point of Sri Lanka. Home to the towering 160-foot white Dondra Lighthouse (the tallest in Southeast Asia), the historic Devinuwara Vishnu Devalaya, and the giant Buddha of Weherahena.

✓ Southernmost Geographic Tip of Sri Lanka
✓ 160ft Colonial Dondra Head Lighthouse & Palms
✓ Weherahena Underground Temple & Giant Buddha
✓ Dutch Star Fort & Matara Pigeon Island Shrine
Private Car & Driver: From $60
Untouched Coast & Blowhole
📍 Hambantota District ⏱️ 1 Hr 40 Mins

🌊 Tangalle, Mawella & Blowhole

Pristine, uncrowded turquoise beaches backed by swaying palm jungles. Witness the Hummanaya Blowhole (the 2nd largest sea blowhole in the world) shoot ocean water 30 meters high, and experience night sea turtle nesting at Rekawa.

✓ Hummanaya Natural Marine Blowhole (Kudawella)
✓ Goyambokka, Silent Beach & Mawella Bays
✓ Rekawa Wild Sea Turtle Night Nesting Project
✓ Mulkirigala Ancient Rock Cave Monastery
Private Car & Driver: From $75
Wild Leopard Safari
📍 Ruhuna / Hambantota ⏱️ 3 Hrs (Full Day)

🐆 Yala National Park Safari

Asia's most famous wildlife safari destination, boasting the one of the world's most renowned natural reserves for wild leopards. Enjoy early morning or afternoon 4x4 open safari jeeps tracking elephants, sloth bears, crocodiles, and exotic birds.

✓ Sri Lankan Leopard Tracking with Tracker Guide
✓ Wild Asian Elephant Herds & Sloth Bears
✓ Private 4x4 Open-Roof Safari Jeep Included
✓ Door-to-Door Private AC Transfer from Hikkaduwa
Full Safari Package: From $120
500+ Wild Elephants
📍 Southern / Sabaragamuwa ⏱️ 2.5 Hrs Drive

🐘 Udawalawe Elephant Safari

Guaranteed sightings of wild Asian elephant families roaming freely across open grassland savannas and water reservoirs. Visit the Elephant Transit Home (ETH) to watch rehabilitated orphan baby calves being bottle-fed milk.

✓ High-Probability Wild Elephant Encounters
✓ Elephant Transit Home Orphan Baby Calf Feeding
✓ 4x4 Open Safari Jeep with Experienced Driver
✓ Scenic Udawalawe Reservoir & Water Birds
Safari & Transfer: From $90
Mangrove Wetland Biosphere
📍 Galle District (North) ⏱️ 25 Mins Drive

🚤 Madu River & Cinnamon Isle

A biodiverse Ramsar wetland sanctuary comprising 64 islands. Cruise through dense mangrove forest tunnels, witness traditional artisan cinnamon peeling on Cinnamon Island, and experience natural river fish therapy.

✓ Private Motorboat Cruise through Mangrove Tunnels
✓ Cinnamon Peeling Craft & Fresh Spice Tasting
✓ Ancient Island Buddhist Temple (Kothduwa)
✓ Kosgoda Sea Turtle Hatchery & Conservation
Private Boat Safari: From $30
UNESCO Tropical Rainforest
📍 Southern Rainforest ⏱️ 2.5 Hrs Drive

🌿 Sinharaja Rainforest Trek

Sri Lanka's last viable primary tropical rainforest and a UNESCO World Biosphere Reserve. Trek through lush green giant canopies, discover secluded waterfalls, and spot endemic birds like the Sri Lanka Blue Magpie with certified naturalists.

✓ Guided Jungle Trekking with Licensed Forest Tracker
✓ Hidden Jungle Waterfalls & Freshwater Pools
✓ Over 95% of Sri Lanka's Endemic Birds & Flora
✓ Private AC Van/Car Transfer Included
Guided Trek Package: From $95
